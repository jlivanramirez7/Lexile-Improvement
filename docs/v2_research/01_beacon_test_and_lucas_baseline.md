## Part 1: DRC BEACON 5th Grade Reading/ELA Assessment (Georgia & Cobb County)

### 1. Purpose & Standards Alignment
**DRC BEACON** (developed by Data Recognition Corporation in partnership with the Georgia Department of Education) is a formative, through-year **computer-adaptive interim assessment** for Georgia students in Grades 3–8. It shares the same **DRC INSIGHT** testing engine and psychometric item bank architecture as the summative end-of-year **Georgia Milestones End-of-Grade (EOG)** assessment.

* **What It Measures (5th Grade ELA/Reading):**
  * **Traditional Georgia Standards of Excellence (GSE) / Milestones Reporting Categories:**
    1. **Reading & Vocabulary:**
       * *Literary Comprehension* (Key Ideas & Details, Craft & Structure, Integration of Knowledge & Ideas in fiction, drama, and poetry).
       * *Informational Comprehension* (cause/effect, text structure, author's claim/evidence, and multi-paragraph synthesis in scientific, historical, and technical texts).
       * *Vocabulary Acquisition & Use* (Tier 2 academic vocabulary in context, figurative language, morphology/affixes, and nuance).
    2. **Writing & Language** (when full ELA battery/testlets are administered; Note: Cobb County elementary schools primarily administer the **Reading Comprehension & Vocabulary** battery to generate official MetaMetrics **Lexile®** measures).
  * **New Georgia K–12 ELA Standards Alignment (Implemented SY 2025–2026+):** Maps across **Texts (T)**, **Language (L)**, **Practices (P)**, and **Foundations (F)** while preserving MetaMetrics Lexile linking and Milestones scale score projections.

### 2. Administration Schedule & Testing Environment in Cobb County (CCSD)
* **Three Official Through-Year Windows:**
  * **Fall Window (August / early September):** Establishes beginning-of-year baseline Lexile/Quantile and routes instructional groups (e.g., Lucas tested `09/04/2025` in G4 and `09/01/2026` in G5).
  * **Winter Window (December / January):** Mid-year growth check and instructional recalibration (e.g., Lucas tested `12/17/2025` in G4).

  * Can be administered as a **Comprehensive Full Battery** (which generates official Lexile/Quantile measures on the Student Report, as indicated by Footnote ² on the 2026 report) or targeted instructional **Testlets**.

### 3. Psychometric Methodology: Computer-Adaptive Testing (CAT)
* **Item-/Passage-Set Adaptive Routing:**
  * Unlike fixed-form tests, DRC BEACON dynamically selects items/passage sets from a calibrated Rasch item pool in real time based on the student's prior responses.
  * Correct answers to on-grade items route the student to higher-Lexile passages (`1000L–1200L+`) and higher Depth of Knowledge (DOK 2–3) items; incorrect answers route to lower-complexity scaffolds to pinpoint ability with minimal testing time.
* **Lexile® & Standard Error of Measurement (SEM) Derivation:**
  * Student ability ($\theta$) is estimated via Item Response Theory (IRT / Rasch model) and linked directly to the **MetaMetrics Lexile® Scale**.
  * Every correct response on a high-difficulty passage/item raises the upper ceiling estimate and narrows the **Standard Error of Measurement (SEM)** (typically converging toward $\pm 15\text{L}$ to $\pm 25\text{L}$ across a full battery).
  * **Critical CAT Implication for High Achievers:** Early careless errors in the first 5–8 items ("routing items") artificially depress the difficulty tier of subsequent passages, capping the maximum achievable Lexile score even if the student answers later items correctly.

### 4. Test Length, Pacing, Question Formats, & DOK Distribution
* **Test Length & Pacing:**
  * **Untimed Benchmark:** Students may take as long as needed within the school testing session.
  * **Typical Duration & Item Count:** Approximately **45–75 minutes** for the Reading battery, typically spanning **~25–35 adaptive items** grouped across **4–6 reading passages** (150–500 words each), plus standalone vocabulary/language items. (In V1/V2 simulator modules, full-length adaptive simulations are condensed into high-density **15-item, 3-stage** CAT trees or **5-item** daily passage workouts).
* **Core Item Types:**
  1. **Standard 4-Option Multiple Choice (MC):** Single best answer (`A, B, C, D`) worth 1 point.
  2. **Two-Part Evidence-Based Selected Response (EBSR — Part A & Part B):**
     * *Part A:* Requires a DOK 2/3 inference, central idea, or analytical claim.
     * *Part B:* Requires selecting the exact verbatim quotation or line evidence from the passage that supports Part A. (Usually worth 2 points total; partial credit may apply only if Part A is correct, making Part A + Part B alignment essential).
  3. **Multi-Select Items:** Requires selecting **two** (or more) correct statements/details from 5–6 options (e.g., *"Which TWO details belong in an objective summary of the passage?"*).
  4. **Technology-Enhanced Items (TEI):** Drop-down inline menus, drag-and-drop sequence/cause-effect tables, or clickable hot-text sentences.
* **Depth of Knowledge (Webb's DOK) Distribution:**
  * **DOK 1 (~15–20%):** Direct textual recall, explicit fact location, basic dictionary/context meaning.
  * **DOK 2 (~50–55%):** Syntactic decoding of complex sentences, Tier 2 vocabulary in context, main idea, cause/effect, character/author perspective, text structure.
  * **DOK 3 (~25–30%):** Cross-paragraph synthesis, author's craft/rhetorical purpose, complex inference paired with verbatim proof (EBSR Part A/B).

### 5. Student Testing Mechanics & UI Tools (DRC INSIGHT Interface)
* **Split-Screen Layout:** Reading passage is anchored in a scrollable left pane with numbered paragraphs (`[1], [2], [3]...`), while the active question(s) appear in the right pane.
* **Universal Digital Tools:**
  * **Pointer / Selection Tool:** Standard cursor for selecting options and interacting with TEIs.
  * **Highlighter:** Highlights key phrases or verbatim evidence in the passage or question stem.
  * **Cross-Off / Option Eliminator (Strikethrough):** Lets students visually strike through ruled-out distractor choices (`A, B, C, D`).
  * **Line Guide:** A draggable horizontal reading bar that isolates one line of text at a time—highly effective for preventing line-skipping inside dense informational paragraphs.
  * **Magnifier / Zoom & Color Contrast / Masking:** Accessibility and visual focus tools.
* **Navigation Rules:** Within a single passage set, students can usually move Back/Next to review items tied to that passage, but once a passage set/stage is submitted to the CAT engine, responses are locked to compute routing for the next stage.
