🎯 Strategy 1: The "Slash and Subject" Deconstruction Drill

When reading non-fiction articles with Lucas Ramirez, ask them to find the main action verb first, then work backward to find who performed it. Have them mentally cross out prepositional phrases starting with by, of, through, during to prevent passive voice confusion.

⚖️ Strategy 2: "The Text is the Law" Proof Requirement

During discussions, if Lucas Ramirez answers with a plausible idea, challenge them with: "Point your finger to the exact line number that proves that." This eliminates the "Outside Knowledge Trap" where bright students pick real-world facts that aren't mentioned in the text.

How DRC BEACON Lexile Scores Are Calculated

The Lexile Framework for Reading uses a dual-variable quantitative psychometric model to evaluate text difficulty and student capability:

1. Syntactic Complexity (Sentence Length & Clauses)

Longer sentences with subordinate clauses, inverted structures, and passive voice require higher working memory to decode subject-verb relationships.

2. Semantic Difficulty (Vocabulary Frequency)

Measures the frequency of Tier 2/Tier 3 academic words across standard reference corpora. Unfamiliar words require context decoding.
